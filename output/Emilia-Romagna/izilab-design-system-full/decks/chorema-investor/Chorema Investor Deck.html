01 · THE CONTEXT

Problems and key questions

Where decision-makers get stuck today — and what Chorema makes decidable.

Where decision-makers get stuck
  • Strategic decisions made under deep uncertainty and rapid change.
  • Fragmented signals that fail to translate into actionable insight.
  • Over-reliance on short-term planning and linear forecasts.
  • Lack of internal foresight capability and shared sense-making.
  • Difficulty aligning strategy, policy, and investment to long-term goals.
What Chorema helps decide
  • How can cities build policies resilient to climate, demographics, and migration?
  • Which weak signals will disrupt specific sectors in the next 5–15 years?
  • How robust is a company's strategy across different plausible futures?
  • Where to invest today to stay competitive tomorrow?
  • Which early indicators to monitor to anticipate systemic risks?
